Modifications: extracted nine painted exterior mesh objects; excluded non-painted equipment and front bumper; preserved supplied vertex normals; rotated axes and raised the shell 0.15 units; converted to GLB with a neutral double-sided material. No original texture maps shipped.

2026-10-12: recovered four connected B-pillar facing strips from the source's `mesh_31_2` black trim object, grouped into one named mesh per side and rendered with the shell's paint material. These preserve the source's exterior pillar contours; they are visual facings, not verified structural pillar sections. Other components of that trim object (mirrors, antenna and window trim) remain excluded. The revised GLB contains 11 meshes and 56,947 triangles.

This is a stripped exterior visualization asset, not engineering CAD or a complete body-in-white assembly. It does not contain verified inner structure, underbody, welds or flange geometry. No implication of endorsement by Ford, Dürr or the source uploader.

Rebuild: the preparation script (`prepare-ranger.py`) and the supplied source archive stay with the owner; this repository ships only the finished GLB.
